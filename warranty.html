The controlling documents

Your Written Terms Come First

The product specification, manufacturer warranty, YardGuard proposal, and any written workmanship terms provided for your project define the actual coverage.

If website copy and a signed project document ever differ, rely on the project documents and ask us to resolve the difference before proceeding.

Two different responsibilities

Product Coverage Is Not Workmanship Coverage

Keeping these documents separate makes it easier to understand who reviews a claim, which conditions apply, and what evidence may be required.

Manufacturer Product Warranty

This is issued by the manufacturer for the specific turf product. Its duration, covered defects, exclusions, remedies, transfer rules, maintenance requirements, and claim process come from the manufacturer's written document.

Ask for the document associated with the exact product named in your proposal rather than relying on a general website term.

Installation Workmanship Terms

These terms apply to YardGuard's contracted installation scope. The proposal or agreement should identify the workmanship commitment, its duration, exclusions, notice process, inspection process, and available remedy.

Conditions outside the contracted scope—such as third-party damage, later construction, drainage changes, or misuse—may be treated differently.

Before and after installation

Keep a Complete Project Record

Clear records help prevent confusion and make any future product or workmanship review more efficient.

  • Before acceptingReview the named turf product, scope, warranty issuer, coverage period, exclusions, maintenance duties, and remedies.
  • At completionKeep the signed agreement, final invoice, product information, warranty documents, care instructions, and completion photos.
  • During ownershipFollow the supplied care guidance and retain records for repairs, changes, or third-party work near the installation.
  • If there is a concernContact YardGuard with the project address, installation date, description, and clear photos. We will acknowledge the request and explain the applicable review process.

Important: This page explains the documentation process; it does not create, extend, or replace a warranty. Coverage is governed by the written terms supplied for the selected product and contracted installation.
