What does the $499K starting investment include?+

Every commission is custom. The starting investment establishes the scale of a comprehensive design-build engagement and may combine golf-surface construction, site and drainage work, landscape architecture, lighting, technology infrastructure and a purpose-built pavilion. Final scope follows site review.

Can the technology be customized?+

Yes. Launch-monitor, simulator, putting-analysis, audiovisual, lighting, security and control systems are selected around the player, property and desired level of automation.

Is a large property required?+

The program is intended for estate-scale or uniquely suitable properties. Feasibility begins with usable area, access, grading, drainage, setbacks, utilities and surrounding uses.

Can you coordinate with my architect or builder?+

Yes. The golf environment can be coordinated with your architect, landscape architect, builder, pool contractor or renovation team so structures, services and finishes read as one property.
