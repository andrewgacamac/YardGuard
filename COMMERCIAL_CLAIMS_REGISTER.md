# YardGuard Commercial-Claims Substantiation Register

**Purpose:** one working register for factual, comparative, performance, safety,
trust, warranty, and service-level claims that may appear on the YardGuard
website, proposals, ads, sales scripts, or structured data.

**Scope:** active public pages and their machine-readable content. Backup pages,
old strategy documents, and archived campaign copy are not approved marketing
sources and must not be republished without a new review.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-21  
**Register owner:** YardGuard operations/marketing owner  
**Evidence rule:** a claim is **Approved** only when the evidence link or file,
exact product/SKU or service scope, issue and expiry dates, jurisdiction, and
an accountable owner are recorded below. A supplier brochure without a SKU,
revision date, or test method is not sufficient evidence for a performance or
safety claim.

Pricing, ROI assumptions, warranty/financing gates, and release change control
are maintained in [`COMMERCIAL_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`](COMMERCIAL_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md).
Use this register for the claim-level evidence record, and update both files
when an approved source changes.

## Status key

- **Qualified / public:** the current page uses cautious, site- or product-specific wording; it does not make a blanket promise.
- **Held:** no public claim should be made until the evidence and wording are approved.
- **Evidence requested:** the item is open with a specific document or operational record needed.
- **Approved:** evidence is attached and the wording has been reviewed. No P0.5 row is currently marked Approved.

## Approved interim public wording

Until a row is **Approved**, use the following controls:

- Say **“site-specific,” “selected product,” “written proposal,”** or **“available manufacturer documentation”** rather than a universal number, safety conclusion, lifespan, or payback.
- Separate **manufacturer product coverage** from **YardGuard workmanship terms** and attach the documents that apply to the proposed system.
- Describe drainage, heat, pet use, winter performance, and odour as design and care considerations. Do not promise that turf will not flood, stay cool, prevent odour, survive every freeze-thaw event, or be safe if chewed or ingested.
- Do not publish review counts/ratings, project totals, years, insurance/WSIB/membership badges, warranty durations, response times, satisfaction guarantees, or “Most Popular” labels without a dated evidence record.
- Do not use **“non-toxic,” “PFAS-free,” “lead-free,” “antimicrobial,” “100%,” “#1,” “guaranteed,”** or similar absolutes unless the exact scope and evidence are recorded and approved.

## Review workflow and cadence

1. Purchasing or operations collects the source document and maps it to a SKU, service, or legal entity.
2. The claim owner records limitations and drafts the narrowest accurate sentence.
3. A compliance/legal reviewer checks regulated safety, environmental, warranty, comparative, and guarantee wording.
4. Marketing updates visible copy, metadata, JSON-LD, FAQ schema, `public/llms.txt`, proposals, and sales scripts together.
5. QA runs the prohibited-absolute scan and checks that every public claim has a register ID.
6. Operations schedules expiry reminders and reopens the row whenever a supplier, SKU, price, contract, or service capacity changes.

Review all open rows monthly while evidence is being gathered, at least quarterly
thereafter, and immediately before a product launch, municipality campaign, or
paid advertisement. Expired, superseded, or unverifiable documents move to
**Retired**; the associated public wording is removed or qualified.
