# Golf Learn research register

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

## Editorial scope

The public page `golf-learn.html` is a Southern Ontario decision framework and source
navigator for private golf environments. It is not a project specification or a
substitute for legal, engineering, architectural, agronomic, municipal, Conservation
Authority or manufacturer advice.

Evidence is classified on the page as:

- **Authority:** law, regulator, government or municipal source.
- **Technical practice:** USGA, ASGCA, university, government technical guidance or
  clearly identified trade-body guidance.
- **Manufacturer:** current product-specific planning information.
- **Project decision:** a value that must be established for the property and recorded
  in the brief, drawings, specification or written proposal.

Professional golf-course evidence is not automatically represented as residential
performance evidence. Manufacturer and trade-association sources are not represented
as independent verification.

## Source register

### Golf architecture and putting-green construction

- USGA, “Beneath the Surface”: researched natural-green construction method and
  system layers.
  https://www.usga.org/content/usga/home-page/articles/2018/02/decades-of-research-fuel-new-specs-for-putting-greens.html
- USGA, construction recommendation revision: material testing, perimeter drainage,
  cleanouts and quality-control changes.
  https://www.usga.org/course-care/turfgrass-and-environmental-research/research-updates/2018/usga-putting-green-construction-recommendations--revision-proces.html
- USGA, “Not All Sand-Based Greens Are USGA Greens”: terminology boundary.
  https://www.usga.org/content/usga/home-page/course-care/green-section-record/62/issue-03/not-all-sand-based-greens-are-usga-greens.html
- USGA, backyard putting-green guide: natural versus synthetic decision factors,
  site access, drainage, irrigation and maintenance commitment.
  https://www.usga.org/content/usga/home-page/course-care/green-section-record/62/issue-17/how-to-make-your-backyard-putting-green-dream-a-reality.html
- ASGCA, “Green Design”: green-complex scope, playability, cuppable areas and
  transitions.
  https://asgca.org/design/existing-courses/course-renovation-articles/green-design/
- ASGCA, “The Master Planning Process”: base information, climate, soils, utilities,
  operations and professional roles.
  https://asgca.org/design/existing-courses/course-renovation-articles/the-master-planning-process/
- ASGCA, “Building a Practical Golf Facility”: safety priority and the absence of a
  single safety standard that replaces site-specific professional judgment.
  https://asgca.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Building-a-Practical-Golf-Facility-final.pdf

### Performance, agronomy and operations

- USGA, green-speed measurement: readings depend on technique, time, weather and
  location.
  https://www.usga.org/content/usga/home-page/course-care/green-section-record/62/issue-12/details-matter-when-measuring-green-speed.html
- USGA, GS3 numbers: use consistent collection and target ranges for speed, firmness,
  smoothness and trueness.
  https://www.usga.org/content/usga/home-page/course-care/green-section-record/62/issue-22/gs3--understanding-the-numbers.html
- USGA, surface organic matter method: representative intact samples, consistent
  timing and laboratory method.
  https://www.usga.org/content/usga/home-page/course-care/green-section-record/62/issue-02/a-standard-method-for-measuring-putting-green-surface-organic-ma.html
- OMAFRA, Integrated Pest Management for Turf: Ontario field reference for diseases,
  insects, weeds and scouting.
  https://www.ontario.ca/page/integrated-pest-management-turf
- University of Guelph, Guelph Turfgrass Institute: Ontario turf research and
  knowledge-transfer facility.
  https://www.uoguelph.ca/alliance/research-facilities/other-research-facilities/guelph-turfgrass-institute

### Southern Ontario climate, drainage and winter

- Environment and Climate Change Canada, 1991–2020 Toronto Pearson climate normals:
  representative station data; not a universal regional design value.
  https://climate.weather.gc.ca/climate_normals/results_1991_2020_e.html?climate_id=6158731
- Ontario, Stormwater Management Planning and Design Manual: integrated water
  balance, water quality, erosion and quantity objectives.
  https://www.ontario.ca/document/stormwater-management-planning-and-design-manual-0
- USGA, Cutten Fields, Guelph: winter injury, surface drainage, shade and annual
  bluegrass case study.
  https://www.usga.org/course-care/water-resource-center/bmp-case-studies/2018/preventing-winter-injury.html
- USGA, Islington Golf Club, Toronto: Southern Ontario winter injury, drainage and
  natural-green rebuild case study.
  https://www.usga.org/course-care/water-resource-center/bmp-case-studies/2017/rebuilding-greens-to-improve-playability-and-address-water-issues.html

### Ontario due diligence and approvals

- Ontario building permit guide: municipal permit administration and common permit
  triggers.
  https://www.ontario.ca/document/citizens-guide-land-use-planning/building-permits
- Ontario One Call homeowner guidance: locate lead time, public locate process and
  private infrastructure examples.
  https://ontarioonecall.ca/homeowners/
- Conservation Ontario authority finder: jurisdiction routing.
  https://conservationontario.ca/conservation-authorities/find-a-conservation-authority
- TRCA planning and permits: regulated areas and property-inquiry process.
  https://trca.ca/planning-permits/
- Ontario water-taking permissions: 50,000-litre-per-day threshold and the need to
  check exemptions or EASR pathways.
  https://www.ontario.ca/page/permits-take-water
- Ontario pesticides and golf courses: IPM registration, accreditation and reporting
  for defined golf courses using unlisted pesticides.
  https://www.ontario.ca/page/pesticides-and-golf-courses
- City of Toronto tree/ravine permits: example of local tree-injury, grading and
  ravine controls.
  https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/building-construction/tree-ravine-protection-permits/how-to-apply-for-a-tree-or-ravine-permit/
- City of Toronto turfgrass and prohibited plants: local soft-landscaping treatment
  of artificial turf; explicitly not generalized to other municipalities.
  https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/public-notices-bylaws/bylaw-enforcement/turfgrass-prohibited-plants/

### Synthetic turf and technology

- Synthetic Turf Council technical guidelines: industry guidance for landscape
  installation, base systems and maintenance. Treated as a trade-body source.
  https://www.syntheticturfcouncil.org/page/guidelines
- TrackMan simulator planning: current manufacturer-specific room guidance for iO
  and TrackMan 4. Dimensions are not generalized to other models.
  https://www.trackman.com/golf/simulator/

## Claims deliberately avoided

- Universal base thickness, drainage capacity, frost depth or compaction target.
- A universal safety setback or claim that netting makes a routing safe.
- A universal green-speed target.
- Universal synthetic-turf lifespan, drainage rate, heat, safety or environmental
  claims.
- Representing a synthetic or generic sand-based green as a “USGA green.”
- Treating Toronto bylaws as Southern Ontario-wide rules.
- Product compatibility, warranty or technology dimensions without the exact current
  manufacturer documentation.

## Review triggers

Review the public page when any of the following occurs:

- a cited Ontario law, ministry page, municipal rule or Conservation Authority
  process materially changes;
- USGA construction recommendations or cited performance methods are revised;
- a cited manufacturer replaces the relevant product or space guidance;
- YardGuard changes its golf service scope or commercial disclosure;
- a reader provides substantiated correction evidence.
